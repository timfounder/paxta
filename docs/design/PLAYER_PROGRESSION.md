# PLAYER PROGRESSION

> Owner of: how the player advances across Nights — the spine of long-term
> growth and what gates the next thing. REWARD_SYSTEM owns *what you earn*; this
> owns *how you progress and why the dread never lets up*.

## 1. The core rule: the player grows, the Custodian does not

PAXTA has **no power progression**. There are no stats to raise, no upgrades, no
buffs, no levels that make Sanity drain slower or anomalies easier. The only
thing that gets better is the **player's own perception and nerve**.

- **R-PROG-1 You never out-level the dread.** Difficulty rises to meet rising
  skill (DIFFICULTY §3), so PAXTA stays frightening at hour 50 as at hour 1. A
  game you can grind into safety is no longer horror (Pillar 1, GDD §4).
- **R-PROG-2 No mechanical character power, ever.** No purchasable or earned buff
  to Sanity, visibility, time, or score multipliers (VISION R1, REWARD R-REW-3).
  Progression is narrative, knowledge, and prestige — never strength.

## 2. The four progression axes

| Axis | What advances | Gate / source | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Narrative** | Nights → Chapters → the story of PAXTA | Completing the prior Night (not score) | STORY §6 |
| **Mastery** | The player's skill: reading kinds/tiers, composure | Practice; implicit | DIFFICULTY §4 |
| **Knowledge** | Case Files: the dossier of wrong things | Correct catches | REWARD §2B |
| **Prestige** | Custodian standing, titles, leaderboard rank | Clean Nights, completion | REWARD §2C |

- **R-PROG-3 Narrative is the primary axis.** The main reason to progress is *what
  happens next in PAXTA*. The other three deepen it.

## 3. Narrative gating (the spine)

- **R-PROG-4 Sequential, completion-gated Nights.** Nights unlock in order; each
  is unlocked by **surviving** the previous one, not by scoring well (REWARD R-REW-9,
  Pillar 4). A struggling player still advances the story.
- **R-PROG-5 Chapters gate softly.** A new chapter unlocks on completing the prior
  chapter's Nights. Chapters 0–1 are free and form a complete arc (VISION §7,
  STORY R-CHP-1); later chapters are the monetised content (REWARD §3, VISION §7).
- **R-PROG-6 Failure delays, never blocks.** Losing a Night means retrying it
  (FAILURE_CONDITIONS R-FAIL-10), not losing access to progression. There is no
  dead end.

## 4. The mastery curve (implicit skill growth)

The player's real progression is **learning to see**. The game shapes this in
three stages, matched by the difficulty curve (DIFFICULTY §3):

| Stage | Nights | The player learns | Game responds |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Induction** | 1–2 | The three verbs; what "normal" is; the visual kind | Gentle tells, tier 1–2, no overlap |
| **Competence** | 3–6 | Audio & environmental kinds; managing overlap; reading tiers | Mixed kinds, shorter tells, tier 3–4 |
| **Mastery** | 7+ / Ch. 2+ | Composure under doubt; rare behaviours; trusting/doubting senses | New behaviours; subtlety; reactive/mimic |

- **R-PROG-7 Teach by playing, not by tutorial.** Each new skill is introduced
  diegetically through a Night that debuts it alone (DIFFICULTY R-DIFF-6, ANOMALY
  R-ANOM-8). No skill trees, no practice menus.
- **R-PROG-8 Mastery reads as calm.** The expert isn't faster — they are *calmer*:
  fewer false alarms, higher Sanity, deliberate movement. The game celebrates this
  (REWARD R-REW-4), making composure the visible mark of progression.

## 5. Knowledge progression (the Case File arc)

Catching each anomaly variant fills its **Case File** (REWARD §2B). Over a
campaign the dossier becomes the player's *visible* record of growth — a horror
encyclopedia they assembled by seeing clearly.

- **R-PROG-9 Knowledge is a progression *and* a teaching loop.** A filled Case
  File reinforces the "correct vs wrong" the player must hold in their head,
  feeding back into mastery (§4). Earned only by correct catches (R-REW-5).

## 6. Prestige progression (the long tail)

**Custodian standing** rises with Clean Nights and chapter completion — a
seniority title and a quiet HUD flourish (REWARD §2C). Leaderboards (Phase 3) add
a competitive tail on equal seeds (REPLAYABILITY R-REPLAY-4).

- **R-PROG-10 Prestige is recognition, not power** (R-PROG-2). It says *who you
  are*, never *what you can do*. A senior Custodian faces the same difficulty as a
  new one.

## 7. Pacing of progression (mobile cadence)

- **R-PROG-11 One Night = one meaningful step.** Each 10–15 min session should end
  with the player *visibly further*: a Night cleared, a Case File or two, a story
  beat, maybe a standing tick. No multi-session unlock walls (Pillar 5).
- **R-PROG-12 No daily-grind progression.** Progress comes from *playing Nights*,
  not from logging in, streaks, or timers (REWARD §5, VISION §8 caution).
- **R-PROG-13 Respect drop-in/drop-out.** A player returning after a week resumes
  exactly where they were (SAVE_SYSTEM); progression never decays with absence.

## 8. Progression design checklist

1. Does it grow the *player* (skill/knowledge/story/prestige), not the *character's
   power* (R-PROG-1/2)?
2. Is the next step gated by completion, not score (R-PROG-4)?
3. Does failure delay, never block (R-PROG-6)?
4. Is any new skill taught by a Night that debuts it alone, no tutorial (R-PROG-7)?
5. Does one session yield one visible step (R-PROG-11)?
6. Is there zero login/streak/timer progression (R-PROG-12)?
